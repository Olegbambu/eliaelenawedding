# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Wedding guests of Elia and Elena: family and friends of every age, including older relatives. Almost all of them open the site on a phone, from a link shared on WhatsApp. They come to find the date and place, confirm attendance, and look at the couple's photos.

## Product Purpose
Single-page wedding website for the wedding of Elia and Elena on 14 May 2027. It tells guests when and where the wedding is, collects RSVPs (deadline 14 April 2027) and shows the IBAN for the honeymoon gift. Success: every guest finds the practical information in seconds on a phone and sends the RSVP without help.

## Positioning
A personal site, not a template: it carries the couple's own constellation illustration, their real photos and their La La Land night-sky theme.

## Operating Context
- Opened mostly on mobile, often on slower connections and older phones.
- Language: Italian.
- RSVP answers arrive by email through Web3Forms.
- Deployed on GitHub Pages from `main`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sections today: hero with countdown, photo album, event details (RSVP form, location with map), gift IBAN.
- Stack: React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS 4, GSAP, Lenis. Static site, no backend.
- Planned new content, to be provided by the couple: the couple's story with dates, the programme of the day, practical info / FAQ. Do not write these facts until the couple provides them.

## Brand Commitments
- La La Land theme is binding: night sky with stars, the couple's constellation illustration (`src/assets/constellation-*.png`), star yellow and purple sunset colours.
- Script headings (Alex Brush) with Lato body text.
- No butterflies (removed November 2026: off-theme). The sky carries the ambient motion: twinkling stars and the occasional shooting star.

## Evidence on Hand
- 19 real photos of the couple in `src/assets/`.
- Constellation illustration for desktop and mobile.
- Confirmed facts: 14 May 2027 at 15:30; Parco della Vittoria, Via Bigolo 17/19, 35010 Trebaseleghe (PD); RSVP by 14 April 2027; dress code "vestirsi colorati"; IBAN IT16X0103036270000000717909.
- No testimonials, schedule, story text or FAQ answers exist yet.

## Product Principles
1. Phone first: everything must work and feel smooth on a mid-range phone.
2. Practical information is never hidden behind decoration.
3. The theme carries the emotion; motion supports it and never slows guests down.
4. Only real facts and real photos from the couple.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Older guests: readable text sizes, high contrast, large tap targets, and respect for reduced-motion settings.
